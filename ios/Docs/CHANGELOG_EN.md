## 1.0.1
More reliable local storage, smoother growth charts, and quieter nighttime polish.

- **Refined Night Lamp & touch details**: Unified soft-vinyl surfaces and warm amber tones across cards, timers, and Growth Book pages, added a gentle card transition when swapping today's play, a bead progress bar for activities, and automatic background pause for ambient scenes to save battery.
- **Upgraded WHO growth curves**: Smoother percentile bands, a toggle between the current stage and the full 0–36 month view, and an interactive strip below the chart to inspect changes since the last measurement and jump straight to editing.
- **Stronger local data & lock protection**: Hardened data sharing between the app and widgets so history is never overwritten, tightened App Lock protection, and improved Lock Screen Live Activity reliability.
- **Cleaner logs & exports**: Exported tables and edited sessions now exclude paused time accurately, and entries with only length or head circumference display cleanly.
- **Stability fixes**: Fixed crashes when setting an end time within the first minute of a timer or importing files with unusual dates or measurements.

## 1.0.0
The first iOS release of Moss & Otto—a quiet companion for playing and growing together from birth to age three.

- **They explore. You hold the light**: Open the app to meet Moss, the curious little explorer, and Otto, the gentle lamp keeping watch nearby; read their story anytime in About Moss & Otto.
- **Today's Play**: One age-matched activity each day on the Home screen with the first step illustrated at a glance, a tap to swap, and a fresh weekly shelf of ideas.
- **37 monthly stations & 46 home activities**: Month-by-month guides from 0 to 36 months, 46 step-by-step activities with 146 hand-made 3D soft-vinyl illustrations, six developmental areas, topics, and search.
- **Optional care journal**: Log feeds, sleep, diapers, and more with timers that track pauses and side switches separately, plus timelines, daily summaries, WHO growth curves, Live Activities, and widgets.
- **Quiet companions**: Moss and Otto never speak—they respond gently with a glance or a nod when you save a log. Choose either as your lead companion; every feature stays the same.
- **Private by design**: No account, no network connection, and everything stays on your device, accompanied by clear privacy, terms, and disclaimer documents.
