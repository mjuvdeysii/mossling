## 1. Overview

1. This app does not require an account. Information you enter is processed solely on your device. The developer does not collect, access, or upload any personal information. This app contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or other third-party SDKs.
2. All information you record in this app is stored exclusively on your own device and cannot be accessed by the developer.
3. This app makes no network connections. App updates are delivered through the App Store.
4. Because this app offers no accounts, account registration and deletion do not apply. To delete your personal information, see Section 4.7 and Section 8.

## 4. Children's Personal Information Protection Rules

1. This app is intended for adult parents and other caregivers. It is not directed at children and is not meant to be operated by children. A child's information may be entered only by a guardian after agreeing to these rules.
2. Children's personal information involved: date of birth, nickname (optional), sex (optional), weight, length or height, head circumference, care logs (feeding, sleep, diapers, solids, baths, temperature, medication, and notes), and "Firsts" entries. Information such as temperature and medication constitutes health information and, together with other information about children under 14 years of age, is sensitive personal information. This app obtains separate guardian consent via a confirmation card on first launch before saving any of this information.
3. Purpose and manner of processing: used solely on your device to calculate age in months, show age-appropriate content, plot growth curves, summarize care logs, and power Home Screen widgets, Lock Screen widgets, and Live Activities. It is never used for profiling, ad targeting, or advertising. Necessity of processing: without a date of birth the app cannot display age-specific content, and without measurements or care logs it cannot plot growth curves or generate summaries; if you choose not to enter this information, you can still browse all content in the app. Impact on personal rights: because all data stays on your device, neither the developer nor any third party can access it; however, anyone with access to your device may view it, so we recommend enabling "App Lock" and setting a device passcode.
4. Storage location and retention period: stored solely on your device (see Section 2 for locations; if iCloud Backup is enabled, app data is included in your device backup as described in Section 6) until the guardian deletes it, clears all data, withdraws consent, or uninstalls the app. The developer operates no servers, does not obtain this information, and does not transfer it to any third party or outside your jurisdiction.
5. Security measures: data is isolated in the system app sandbox and encrypted with iOS Data Protection; Lock Screen Live Activities never display a child's nickname, temperature, or medication; you can enable "App Lock" protected by Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode. Exported files remain protected by iOS Data Protection while on your device, but the files themselves are not separately encrypted and are no longer protected by this app once sent or copied off your device—please keep them safe. To protect against device loss, we recommend keeping a Lock Screen passcode enabled.
6. Consequences of refusal: if you do not agree to these rules, you can still browse all activities and monthly guides, but you cannot enter a child's profile or care logs. You may choose to consent at any later time.
7. Access, copy, correction, deletion, and withdrawal of consent: tap "Edit" in "Studio" to view or update your child's profile; under "Lamp," tap an entry to edit or delete it; in "Studio › Local Data," you can export a full backup (to view, copy, or transfer your data), tap "Clear All Data" to delete all information, or tap "Withdraw Consent" to withdraw consent and delete all information at the same time. All of these actions happen immediately on your device without needing to contact the developer.
8. Responsible person and complaints: the developer ({{DEVELOPER}}) is responsible for children's personal information protection and can be reached at {{CONTACT}}. See Section 10 for complaint channels.
9. If there are material changes to these rules, we will notify you prominently in the app and request guardian consent again.
10. This app processes the information above in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China and the Provisions on the Cyber Protection of Children's Personal Information.

## 5. Third-Party Sharing, Entrusted Processing, and Cross-Border Transfers

This app does not share, transfer, entrust for processing, or publicly disclose personal information to any third party, contains no third-party SDKs, and does not transfer personal information across borders. When you tap an external link in "Studio › Legal & Help" or "Sources," the corresponding website opens in the system browser and handles your visit under its own privacy rules.

## 6. System Backups

If iCloud Backup is turned on for your device, iOS automatically encrypts and backs up this app's data to your own iCloud account as part of your device backup and restores it when you switch devices. This process is handled entirely by iOS, not by this app. You can manage it in Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › iCloud Backup.

## 7. User-Initiated Actions

- **Exporting records (full JSON backup, Markdown clinician/AI summary, CSV spreadsheet, Clinic Snapshot PDF, 3-Year Growth Yearbook PDF)**: files are generated on demand and handed directly to the system Save/Share sheet rather than left exposed in an unencrypted Files app folder. Exported files contain sensitive personal information about your child (JSON backups, Markdown summaries, CSV tables, and Clinic Snapshot PDFs exclude raw photos and `.m4a` voice recordings; only the 3-Year Growth Yearbook PDF embeds up to 4 selected Firsts photo thumbnails when you explicitly export the Yearbook, while keeping unopened Time Capsule letters masked); once shared with another person or app (including an AI assistant), that recipient handles the data under its own rules.
- **Importing a backup file**: the app reads only the single backup file you select in the system file picker (previously exported by this app), writes it locally, and keeps no extra copy of the file.
- **Sharing an activity or web handover/family card**: only when you tap "Share" or generate a no-download web beacon or family card is the activity text or `#d=` URL-fragment link handed to the app you select. Family exploration cards omit your child's name by default and share only stage/activity highlights; Handover Beacons include basic nickname, age, and care summary (with fever and medication details masked). Payloads use unencrypted base64url encoding strictly inside the `#d=` URL fragment with zero server upload, though opening the link visits the static site host, messaging apps may fetch link previews, and anyone possessing the URL can decode the fragment. Share only with trusted family members.
- **Home Screen widgets & Live Activities**: display your child's age, active timers, and recent logs using data stored locally on your device, without passing through any server.
- **Clearing all data**: in "Studio › Local Data," tapping "Clear All Data" and confirming deletes your child's profile, all care logs, settings, the widget copy, and private temporary/export caches (`Application Support/Exports` and temp files) on this device, and immediately ends any active timer on the Lock Screen. Files already sent elsewhere are not affected.

## 8. Deleting Your Data

You can tap "Clear All Data" or "Withdraw Consent" in "Studio › Local Data," or delete the app from your device. Deleted data cannot be recovered.
