# TestFlight Release Notes

## 2.0
Welcome to the TestFlight beta of Moss & Otto v2.0 — a calm, 100% offline companion for families with children aged 0–3, available in English and Simplified Chinese.

- **Four-Tab Spatial Home & Golden Bulb**: Explore focuses on daily play, Sensory Toybox, and First Foods; Lamp centers Otto's bedside light, 6 soundscapes, and 24-hour rhythm; Keepsakes gathers the 37-station trail, Firsts keepsake prints, Time Capsules, and WHO growth curves; Studio holds preferences, Atelier wallpapers, and privacy controls. Pull or tap Otto's golden bulb to toggle the lamp or watercolor morph between 3D soft-vinyl and 2D paper-cut art.
- **46 Bespoke Co-Play Mini-Stages (0–36m)**: Step-by-step illustrated play guides with hands-free playback, smooth wooden spine scrubbing, collector wallpaper export, and a dim Screen-Calm mode.
- **Sensory Toybox & Ear-Safe Check**: Rosewood Xylophone (3 timbres, 6 melodies), Golden Bead Rainstick, Firefly Lantern Jar, 12 Contrast Cards, and Otto's Optical Shadow Wall with passive ear-safe volume guidance.
- **Bedside Lamp & 3AM One-Hand Sanctuary**: 6 offline soothing soundscapes (with dual-layer mixing & timer fade), 3 warm color temperatures, silent CoreHaptics palm-pat pacing, and an auto 00:00–04:59 zero-glare night logging mode.
- **Preterm Dual-Track & On-Device Privacy**: Gestational age (24–36 wks) corrected-age support, CN/US/UK/AU well-child guides, App Lock protection extending across Home Screen widgets, Control Center, and Siri, plus local PDF yearbook and JSON/CSV exports.

### What to Test on Real Devices
- Bedside lamp slide-to-dim gesture, auto-fade countdown into sleep, and Lock Screen Live Activity sync.
- App Lock shielding across foreground/background transitions, Home Screen widgets, Control Center toggles, and Siri queries.
- Unplugging headphones during soundscape playback (verifying quiet auto-stop instead of speaker burst).
- Multi-side nursing undo, Care Mode, serverless web handover/card links, and local backup round-trip.
